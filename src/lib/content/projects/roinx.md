ROINX turns sports predictions into a social game with a public record. Players publish Picks before kickoff; results settle after the event, making performance visible over time instead of relying on winning screenshots.

## Make every Pick count

Each Pick includes a selection, reasoning, simulated stake and user-reported odds. Settled results build each Player's profile and leaderboard record. Prediction Score, Reputation and ROI appear alongside the number of settled Picks, so performance has context.

Follow Players, compare their history, discuss matches, and save Picks to a Tip Slip. Groups and competitions give fans more ways to take part.

## For Players and creators

Creators can build an audience around their analysis with subscriptions and individual Pick unlocks. Picks use simulated stakes only: ROINX takes no wagers and is not a bookmaker.
